Start with the data

Which chart fits?

A diameter measurement, a failed clip, and a surface mark provide different kinds of information. Identify what is recorded before selecting a chart.

Instructions

Calculate, examine, explain.

Begin with the process

Read the case and identify the data type. Keep reference samples separate from later monitoring samples.

Make the chart your own

Enter your calculation and see the resulting chart. Feedback helps you revise the calculation and identify the evidence.

Make a defensible decision

Explain whether investigation is warranted and which records you would examine. A signal is evidence, not proof of a cause.

Suggested sequence: SPC foundations → variable charts (Chapter 6) → attribute charts (Chapter 7) → EWMA and CUSUM (Chapter 9). Your instructor will assign the relevant lab.

Work can be completed with a calculator, Excel, or Minitab. Each lab offers raw-data download, concise feedback, and a reflection for class discussion. Answers remain on the current page and clear when it is refreshed.
